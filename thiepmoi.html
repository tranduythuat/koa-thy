The Destination

We've always believed the best celebrations bring people together around good food, beautiful places, and the ones they love most. We can't wait to share them with you.

Vietnam is where Thy's story began, and where our two families first came together. It reflects so much of who we are: rich in culture, full of adventure, and never far from the sea. Hoi An is its most enchanting corner: a lantern-lit ancient town, lush tropical gardens, a golden stretch of coastline, and quite possibly the best food you'll ever eat. It has everything we love in one place.

For many of you, this will be your first time in Vietnam, and it makes us so happy that we get to share this beautiful corner of the world with you.

Palm Garden Beach Resort & Spa, where lush greenery meets the sea, just moments from Hoi An Ancient Town.

Find it here: Palm Garden Resort Hoi An

One of the kindest months of the year. Expect warm, sunny days around 24–26°C, with cooler evenings of 19–21°C.

Think linen, sandals, sunscreen, and a light layer for after sunset.

The closest airport is Da Nang International Airport (DAD), approximately 45 minutes from Palm Garden. From Australia, there are easy connections via Ho Chi Minh City, Hanoi, Singapore, Kuala Lumpur or Hong Kong. Once you land, Grab (Vietnam's version to Uber) is a cheap and reliable way to get around. If you'd prefer, we can help arrange private airport tranfers.

Most nationalities can apply for a Vietnam e-Visa online at https://evisa.gov.vn - allow 3-4 weeks to be safe.

The Travel Guide
MAKE A HOLIDAY OF IT - HOI AN REWARDS A FEW EXTRA DAYS.

Stay with us at Palm Garden Beach Resort & Spa for beachfront views, beautiful pools, and the convenience of being just steps from the celebrations. Prefer the charm of the Old Town? You'll find plenty of boutique hotels just 10–15 minutes away. As February is high season, we'd recommend booking early.

To book your stay and secure the best rate, you can email your booking request directly to the resort at: 💌 reservation@pgr.com.vn (Subject line: Thy & Koa Wedding)

Bánh mì Phượng (the bánh mì that made Anthony Bourdain swoon), Morning Glory Original (Hoi An classics done right), cao lầu (the noodle dish you truly eat in Hoi An), Nữ Eatery (tiny, modern Vietnamese) - and fresh seafood with your toes in the sand at An Bang Beach.

Start your day with a Vietnamese coffee, whether it's on a rooftop overlooking Hoi An's yellow walls at Faifo Coffee, amongst the rice paddies at Roving Chillhouse or Tok Bar & Restaurant, or by the beach at The Deck House. As you wander the lantern-lit streets, don't miss a cup of Mót, Hoi An's iconic lemongrass herbal tea. Come sunset, head to An Bang Beach for a drink by the sea, or end the evening with cocktails at one of our favourites: Mezcal Cocteleria, Mộng Nguyệt Cocktail Bar, or Hèm Hoi An.

Post-flight massages are practically mandatory here. Hoi An is home to countless beautiful spas, making it the perfect place to unwind after your journey, with highly recommended options including Serene Spa, White Rose Spa, La Luna Spa, La Spa Hoi An.

Known as Vietnam's tailoring capital, Hoi An is the perfect place to have something made, whether it's a custom suit, a dress, or your Funky Formal look. Many tailors can complete garments within 24–48 hours, with Bebe Tailor, Yaly Couture, and A Dong Silk among the most well-known. Arriving a couple of days before the wedding will give you plenty of time for fittings and final adjustments.

Wander the lantern-lit streets of the Old Town after dark, drift through the coconut palms on a basket boat, take a Vietnamese cooking class, or watch the sunrise over Mỹ Sơn Sanctuary. And if you have the chance, make time for the iconic Hải Vân Pass - our favourite adventure in Central Vietnam.
