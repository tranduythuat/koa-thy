THE WEDDING OF Koa and Thy
The Story

It all began at a birthday party in Sydney in 2019.

A cancelled after-party turned into hours of conversation by the beach, and the beginning of everything that followed.

Since then, it's been road trips, sunset picnics, concerts and festivals, adventures abroad, and creating a home together in Sydney. In January 2026, we returned to the lookout where we'd watched one of our very first sun- sets, and Koa got down on one knee.

Our next chapter continues in Hoi An, and we can't wait to celebrate it with all of you.
